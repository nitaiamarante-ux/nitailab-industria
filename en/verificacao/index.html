What I do not do

I do not issue technical reports or legal opinions. I work alongside the geologist, the laboratory and the lawyer, and cover what falls between them: operations and logistics.

Frequently asked questions

Does this replace legal due diligence?

No. It complements it on the technical and logistics side. The lawyer looks at contracts and liabilities; I look at whether the mine exists on paper and on the ground, whether it produces what it claims and whether the cargo can reach the port.

Do you issue technical reports?

No. Quality and reserve reports belong to the laboratory and the geologist. I read the report and explain what it means for logistics and for price.

How long does it take?

A quick assessment takes a few days. The full dossier depends on how many documents the other party provides.

Is this useful for someone outside Brazil?

Yes. Foreign buyers and investors usually ask for exactly this before making any offer.
