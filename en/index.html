How I work

Working principles

Everything in writing first

Scope, timeline and fee agreed before work starts. No retainers without a defined deliverable and no boilerplate reports.

Confidentiality both ways

Contacts and information shared in confidence are not passed on. I propose a non-disclosure and non-circumvention agreement that protects both parties and the introducer.

A conversation before a proposal

The first conversation about your case is free of charge. If there is work to do, we agree on the format together. If there is not, you leave with an objective assessment.

An initial assessment of your case

Describe the cargo, the operation or the deal in a few lines. I will reply with a first assessment and the recommended next step.
