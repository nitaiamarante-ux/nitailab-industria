The numbers

The price at origin is what is left after every leg

The buyer pays the reference price minus everything between the origin and the ship's hold. Anyone who does not know each leg is negotiating blind.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work only with iron ore?

No. I have worked with iron ore, grains, soybean meal, fertilisers, pig iron, steel products and fuels. The logic is the same: every leg has a cost, a lead time, a capacity and an owner.

What is netback?

It is what is left for the origin after paying every leg up to the buyer: road freight, rail, terminal, port, ocean freight, taxes and working capital. It is the minimum price that makes the deal work at the mine or the farm.

Do you contract freight or handle the cargo yourself?

I am not a carrier or a freight forwarder. I design the chain, compare alternatives, coordinate the operators and track the KPIs on the cargo owner's behalf.

Do you work with foreign companies?

Yes. For those buying, selling or investing in Brazil from abroad, I act as your eyes on the ground: who operates what, where the cargo gets stuck and who to talk to.
